  1. ◎
    The Signal Sigil

    The charged circle at the floor's centre. Touch it to draw a Spark — a prompt pairing a figure, mood, medium, and compositional rule through a neo-shamanic cyberpunk lens. Hit draw again to remix the mood and glitch. Every result is ready for your prompt sheet or sketchbook.

  2. ◫
    The Ghost Mirror

    Unlocks after your first Spark. Touch the tall mirror on the right wall to reveal a Character Ghost — a named archetype carrying a trait and a story hook shaped by street ritual, machine hauntings, and signal trance. Use them to anchor the painting, or let the spark drift on its own.

  3. ◈
    The Relic Cache

    Also unlocks after your first Spark. Touch the glowing vessel beneath the mirror to open the Relic Cache. Common drops shape palette or composition. Rare drops bend the direction. Mythic drops open a full challenge thread or story cycle.

  4. ⎘
    Copy Icons

    Every generated line keeps a small clipboard icon. Click it to copy clean text — no HTML, no extra characters — straight to your notes, prompt sheet, or painting plan. It turns green to confirm.
