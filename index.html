  1. ◎
    The Rug Sigil

    The glowing circle at the floor's centre. Touch it to draw a Spark — a creative prompt pairing a subject, mood, medium, and compositional rule. Hit draw again to remix the mood and twist. Every result is paste-ready for any AI generator or prompt sheet.

  2. ◫
    The Mirror

    Unlocks after your first Spark. Touch the tall mirror on the right wall to reveal a Character Guide — a named archetype carrying a trait and a story hook. Use them to anchor the painting, give it a subject, or ignore them entirely and let the Spark run free.

  3. ◈
    The Orb

    Also unlocks after your first Spark. Touch the glowing sphere above the mirror to open the Inspiration Vault. Common drops give palette or compositional twists. Rare adds a wild redirect. Legendary drops a full session challenge or series seed.

  4. ⎘
    Copy Icons

    Every piece of generated text has a small clipboard icon. Click it to copy clean text — no HTML, no extra characters — straight to your generator, notes app, or prompt sheet. It turns green to confirm.
